## The useful result: a testable electrostatic hypothesis

Position 126 sampled across charge classes, which sets up a real experiment
rather than a blind ranking:

**Terephthalate carries −2 at pH 7.4; hippurate carries −1.** A positively
charged residue in the pocket should therefore stabilize the dianion *more* than
the monoanion, widening the very margin Day 7 identified as the problem. A
negatively charged residue should do the opposite.

This yields a falsifiable prediction for Day 9:

> margin(N126R/K) > margin(N126 neutral) > margin(N126D/E)

| Class | Variants | Prediction |
|---|---|---|
| **Positive** (R/K) | ss09, ss13, ss16, ss28 | widen TPA–hippurate margin |
| Neutral (M/L/Q) | ss01, ss03, ss05, ss06, ss10, … | ≈ WT margin |
| **Negative** (D/E) | ss14, ss21, ss24 | narrow margin — **negative controls** |
| WT Asn retained | ss02, ss04, ss08, ss20, ss23, ss27 | internal reference |

Built-in positive and negative controls make this far stronger than ranking 30
variants and reporting the best. If the predicted ordering holds, it is evidence
the pipeline is capturing real physics. If it does not, that is informative
about the scoring function — see the caveat below.

---

## ⚠️ Caveat that directly limits Day 9

**Vina's default scoring function has no explicit electrostatic term.** It is
empirical — steric, hydrophobic, and hydrogen-bond terms keyed on atom types
(this is the same fact recorded on Day 6 that made the Gasteiger charge sum
irrelevant).

Consequences for the hypothesis above:

- Vina **will** partially see an Arg/Lys at 126, because its NH groups are typed
  `HD` donors and TPA's carboxylate oxygens are `OA` acceptors — the hydrogen-bond
  half of a salt bridge is captured.
- Vina **will not** see the net-charge component, which is precisely what
  distinguishes a −2 ligand from a −1 ligand at range.

So Vina is expected to **underestimate** any selectivity gain from charge
complementarity. This biases conservative: an improvement Vina *does* report is
likely real; an absence of improvement is weak evidence, not disproof.

Vina 1.2.7 offers `--scoring ad4`, which does include electrostatics, but it
requires precomputed AutoGrid affinity maps and AutoGrid is not installed.
`vinardo` is available without maps but is also charge-free. **If the charge
hypothesis turns out to matter, installing AutoGrid for AD4 rescoring of the
top variants is the correct follow-up** — worth flagging in the Day 14 pitch as
a known methodological gap rather than papering over it.

## What this library is and isn't

✅ Preserves every direct carboxylate contact — primary TPA recognition intact
by construction.
✅ Varies the second shell, where TPA-vs-hippurate steric discrimination lives.
✅ Contains explicit positive and negative controls for a mechanistic hypothesis.
✅ Verified position mapping with a hard integrity check.

❌ Still generated by a ligand-blind model — MPNN chose these substitutions for
foldability, not binding. The charge diversity at 126 is a fortunate byproduct
of sampling, not something MPNN was optimizing for.
❌ Effective design space is 3 positions. This is a small, focused library, not
a broad exploration.
❌ N205→G/A (25/30) and F252→I/V (30/30) still delete polarity and aromatic
bulk. Those may still hurt binding; Day 9 tests it.

---

## Recommended Day 9 scope

Dock these 30 second-shell variants against the full 7-metabolite panel.
Rank by **TPA-minus-hippurate margin**, not absolute TPA score.

Include WT as the reference row, and check the N126 charge-class ordering
before looking at any individual variant — that ordering is the result most
likely to generalize.
